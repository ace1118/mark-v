How to Use MARK V

👋 Welcome to MARK V

Mobile Analysis, Recording, and Key Validation. Check bubble answer sheets with your phone's camera, score them instantly with DepEd-style analytics, and keep everything - fully offline, nothing uploaded.

Quick Start Answer Keys Printing Scanning Classes Reports QR & Sharing Your Data FAQ

🚀 Quick Start

  1. Build a test in Setup: type the name, number of items, and choices, then tap in the answer key.
  2. Tap Save to Library - the test becomes ACTIVE and ready for scanning.
  3. Print, Download, or Share the answer sheet, then photocopy one per pupil.
  4. After the test, open Scan, snap each sheet, pick or type the pupil's name, and tap Save and Scan Another.
  5. Open Reports for scores, item analysis, and mastery - or Saved to revisit and export any past test.
Tip: set up your Class rosters first (see below) so names are ready to tap while scanning instead of typing each one.

🔑 Answer Keys & SOLO Scoring

Tap a choice to mark it. Each item can hold one 2-point and one 1-point answer:

Tap 1×1 point - the correct answer for a regular item, or the partial answer on a SOLO item.
Tap 2×2 points (dark, with a "2" badge) - the best answer on a SOLO item.
Tap 3×Clears the choice.

Number of Items counts the items printed on the sheet. In By points numbering, each 2-point item spans two numbers (1-2, 3, 4, 5-6…) the way DepEd SOLO papers do - so 20 items with two 2-pointers is 18 questions. The note beside the field shows this live.

Numbering only changes the printed labels - bubble positions stay the same, so sheets you already printed still scan correctly.

Your work here auto-saves as a draft - closing the app never loses it. Saving to the library stores it permanently and makes it the active test.

🖨️ Printing Answer Sheets

  • Always print from this app. Scanning depends on the exact layout - especially the four black corner squares. Don't redraw or crop them.
  • Print at 100% scale (no "fit to page"). Photocopies of a printed master are fine.
  • Sheet Layout: choose 1 per page (Letter) or 2 per A4 - two sheets on one A4 with a cut line, halving your paper cost. Cut them apart; both scan the same.
  • Download saves a PDF; Share sends it via Messenger, Gmail, Drive, etc. - handy when the printer is at a computer shop.
Reprint if you change the item count or choices - old sheets won't match the new bubble grid.

📷 Scanning Sheets

  • Lay the sheet flat, fill the frame, keep all four corner squares visible, and avoid strong shadows.
  • Any orientation works - portrait, landscape, even upside-down. The app detects it.
  • Pupils must shade bubbles fully and darkly. A light mark or a circled letter may not register.
  • The handwritten name from the sheet is cropped and shown above the name box, so you can read and type it without picking the paper back up.
  • Auto capture: the live camera snaps by itself the moment it sees all four corner squares steady - just move sheet after sheet under the phone. Turn it off with the Auto button if you prefer tapping Capture. A Light button appears on phones with a flash for dim rooms.
  • Misread a mark? Tap the correct bubble right on the result picture to override it (tap the marked bubble again to clear it), then save. No rescan needed.
  • Pick a class and MARK V shows who is still missing for the test - tap a missing name to fill it in. Saving the same pupil twice asks whether to replace or keep both, and every save has an Undo.

After each scan, teal squares and dots show exactly where the app looked - they should sit on the corner squares and bubbles. Colored rings mark each answer by points earned:

Goldthe 2-point best answer was chosen
Greena 1-point answer was chosen
Redwrong - no credit
Dottedthe correct choice(s) on any item not fully credited

Save Record saves and waits; Save and Scan Another saves and reopens the camera; Try Again retakes the same sheet.

📊 Reports & Analytics

Everything is computed live from your scans, following DepEd assessment standards:

  • Class stats - MPS, average, highest/lowest, passers, and KR-20 reliability.
  • Item analysis - per item: full-credit %, mastery level, a plain-language action, difficulty, discrimination (D-index, top vs. bottom 27%), and a Retain / Revise / Reject verdict. Sorted least-mastered first.
  • Competency analysis - add each learning competency with the items that measure it (e.g. 1-4, 7); mastery is computed per competency and sorted least-mastered first. Define these against real data anytime - no setup needed beforehand.
  • Consolidate multiple tests - tick several tests (e.g. all your Science sections) for one combined report.

Mastery bands (per DepEd): High Mastery 76–100%, Nearly Mastered 51–75%, Least Mastered 0–50%.

At the bottom of Reports, Export or Share one comprehensive CSV - summary, scores, item analysis, and competencies in a single file.

🔗 QR Codes, Sharing & Backup

  • QR on every sheet: each printed sheet carries a small QR (top-right) holding the answer key. On another phone, tap Scan QR in Setup and point the camera - the whole test loads with no re-typing.
  • One test, many classes: after a test already has scans, scanning its QR again makes a numbered copy (e.g. "EPP Term 1 (2)") so each class stays in its own report group. Later, use Consolidate to see them together.
  • Move a test between devices: in Saved, tap Export Data or Share Data to get a .markv file with the test, all its scans, and competencies. On the other phone, tap Import MARK V Data - everything is restored. If it already exists there, you choose to import a copy or merge the new scans in.
CSV vs .markv: CSV is the readable report you open in Excel. The .markv file is a full backup that only MARK V opens but restores a working test - great for reinstalls or new phones.

🔒 Your Data & Privacy

Everything - answer keys, drafts, classes, and scan records - is stored only on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and the app works fully offline.

Back up before clearing. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage erases your records. Export important tests (CSV or .markv) first - and the .markv files double as backups you can re-import anytime.

App Lock: on the Saved tab you can set an optional 4-6 digit PIN that MARK V asks for every time it opens - sensible protection for pupil records on a personal phone. There is no recovery for a forgotten PIN, so choose one you'll remember.

Archive vs Delete: Archiving hides a finished test to keep the library tidy - its data stays in Saved and Restore brings it back. Only Delete removes data permanently.

❓ Quick Answers

A scan misread a faint mark - what do I do?

Ask pupils to shade fully and darkly. If it still misreads, tap Try Again and retake with less shadow and the sheet flatter. The teal dots show where the app looked - they should land on the bubbles.

Can I scan without a class set up?

Yes - pick "no class" and just type the name. Classes only add name suggestions and auto-fill the section.

Why did scanning a QR make a second copy of my test?

Because that test already had scans. MARK V keeps each class separate by making a numbered copy - so your report groupings stay clean.

Do the two-up A4 sheets scan the same?

Yes. Just cut along the dashed line first; each half works exactly like a full sheet.

Will the live QR scanner work if I open the file from Downloads?

The live camera needs a secure context (the packaged app, or an https/localhost page). Opened straight from Downloads it falls back to a single photo of the QR, which also works.
